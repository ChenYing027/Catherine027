CHT2003 · SOLO VIDEO PROJECT

20-Minute Park Effect

This is my individual class short, reflecting on the constant stress and quiet pressure we experience in daily life. The film explores how twenty minutes immersed in nature — a park, green space — can ease our anxious minds. Using self-shot footage, I experiment with camera work and editing rhythm to share this gentle, personal observation.

  • Role Direction, camera, editing
  • Tools Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve
  • Year 2025
